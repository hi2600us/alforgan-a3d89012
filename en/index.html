Our mission

To enable Quran education and memorization for women and children in Balqarn Governorate, and to support the upkeep and maintenance of mosques — through a self-funding charitable endowment that does not depend on recurring donations.

Alforgan Charitable Endowment & Women's Quran Memorization House is a registered nonprofit organization, officially registered with the General Authority of Awqaf in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Registration ID 1112506318, based in Afra, Balqarn Governorate, Asir Region.

Our story

The founder of this endowment

This blessed edifice was founded by the grace of God, and then through the vision and generous hand of Sheikh Abdullah bin Saad bin Hanash Al-Qarni (may God have mercy on him), who embodied the highest meanings of giving and benevolence when he endowed this building.

He left behind an inexhaustible legacy for the community and a journey of goodness whose rewards extend to the Day of Judgment — a model to be followed in relieving hardship and loving good for society.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask us

What is Alforgan Charitable Endowment?

Alforgan Charitable Endowment & Women's Quran Memorization House is a registered nonprofit organization (Registration ID 1112506318) in Afra, Balqarn Governorate, Asir Region, Saudi Arabia. It invests in a mixed-use building — a hotel, a Quran memorization nursery, a café, and a mosque — directing its income to the Balqarn Quran Memorization Society and Alforgan Mosque.

What is your mission and what activities do you run?

Our mission is to enable Quran education and memorization for women and children in Balqarn Governorate, and to support the upkeep and maintenance of mosques. We run four activities: operating a women's Quran memorization house; funding the Balqarn Quran Memorization Society; maintaining Alforgan Mosque; and operating income-generating endowment facilities (hotel, café, nursery) that self-fund these activities.

What does "one-time donation" mean here?

Once finishing works are complete and the building is operating, commercial income from the hotel and café will permanently fund Quran memorization and mosque upkeep on its own, with no future donations needed. Your contribution today is a single investment in an ongoing, self-renewing charity.

What phase is the project in right now?

The project is in Phase 2 of 4: the concrete structure is complete, and interior and exterior finishing works are underway. Remaining work is expected to take about one year, God willing.

How can I donate to Alforgan Endowment?

By direct bank transfer to the endowment's account at Al Rajhi Bank. To confirm your transfer and receive a receipt, please contact the trustee board at admin@alforgan.org or +966 50 560 9022.

Where is the Alforgan project located?

The project is located in Afra, Balqarn Governorate, Asir Region, Saudi Arabia, directly beside Alforgan Mosque.

How do I register interest in the Quran memorization circles?

Through the "Quran circles" registration page on the endowment's website. You will be contacted once registration formally opens after the building is completed.
